Boss Codex

Defeat the Two-Faced Demon to unlock the hard bosses in the summon panel, one after another. After The Glitch, three mythic bosses wait at the end of the trials, and beyond them, The Idol.

The King of Adaptation

📍 Shrine of the Eight Spokes

Eight priests sealed the Divine General inside a forest shrine. Every spoke of its wheel remembers a blade that once wounded it.

Switch weapons to break through its adaptation.

Ethernaty

📍 The Prismatic Void

When the first star cracked, light leaked into a fold of space and learned to think. Ethernaty bends every color into a weapon.

Weave between cosmic projectiles.

Fire Destroyer

📍 Caldera of Ashfall

The dragon nested in the oldest volcano and burned the northern kingdoms until only the ice of Hrim still held.

Watch the dragon’s attack warnings.

Solar Devourer

📍 The Sunken Sun Pit

When the old sun fell, a worm fed on its embers for a thousand years and learned to swallow light itself.

Avoid mines and slip between segments.

Two-Faced Demon

📍 Gate of Two Faces

One face judges and the other forgives, never at the same time. It guarded the road to the Sanctuary for an age.

Save your dash for lightning and beams.

Selene VeyHard

📍 The Drowned Observatory

The Sanctuary’s last astronomer tried to close the sky’s eye. The moon hollowed her out, and now the tide obeys her.

Dash outward from gravity wells. Her crescents always return to her hand.

Vael’KhorHard

📍 The Shattered Glacier of Hrim

A king who froze his own kingdom to stop the Fire Destroyer. He still holds his throne, waiting for a war that ended without him.

Blizzards slow you. Look for the gap in every avalanche ring.

HorologueHard

📍 The Clock Spire at World’s End

The artificer who forged the King of Adaptation’s wheel became the pendulum of a clock that counts down to the end of every fight.

When time stops, find the gap in the ring of frozen bolts.

The GlitchHard

📍 The Null Sector

When Horologue’s clock struck midnight, something never written slipped through the gap between the hours: a shadow made of every deleted boss.

Every hit deals 400 damage. Watch for aim lines, red outlines, glowing portals and the marker where it appears next.

KaeltharMythic

📍 The Colosseum of the Black Sun

The undefeated duelist the Architect made the last door of the trials. He has never been allowed to stop fighting.

Lanes show where he will dash, and his path erupts behind him. Find the gap in every wave of falling swords.

NyxaraMythic

📍 The Stormspire Above the Clouds

The six-winged herald who sang the first storms. When the Glitch broke the trials, she began screaming lightning into the silence.

Stand in the dark squares of the judgment grid. Tornadoes pull you in, and ball lightning bursts when it fades.

The ArchitectMythic

📍 The First Page

The author who drew every arena and gave every boss its attacks. It opens the page one last time to write your ending.

It replays its creations’ attacks. The heart of a blueprint star is safe until it ignites. When the margins close in, stay on the page.

The IdolDivine

📍 The Throne Beyond Heaven

Beyond the last sky, something older than the Architect watched every trial. Now that its author has fallen, it rises to judge you itself.

Leave the centre when the Judgement Beam gathers. Hold the Infinity Edge beam between you and a glitch laser to block it. Stand in the middle when lightning crawls along the edges, and cross the cut before half the world falls.
